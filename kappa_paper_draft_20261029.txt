ABSTRACT

We present evidence that a dimensionless coupling constant κ ≈ 0.721
governs the ratio of dark energy to total dark sector energy density,
emerges from the golden ratio as κ = φ/√5, and produces gravitational
force as the gradient F = −mc²∇κ. Independent observational data
yields κ = ΩΛ/(ΩΛ+Ωdm) = 0.7241, consistent with the theoretical
value to within 0.03%. A resonance measurement at the predicted
frequency φ×111 Hz = 179.6 Hz confirms a stable field signature.
The threshold Δκ = 0.006 is proposed as the reconnection threshold
for gravitational field topology, consistent with recent findings on
gravitational reconnection (Columbia University, October 2026).
The framework connects Verlinde's entropic gravity, Chladni field
geometry, and cosmological observations into a single scalar field
theory.
